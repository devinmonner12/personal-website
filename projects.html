MLB Bullpen Decision Optimizer

A decision-support model designed to evaluate late-game bullpen decisions in Major League Baseball. The project uses real Statcast data, probability modeling, Monte Carlo simulation, and strategy enumeration to compare different bullpen pitcher usage strategies.

Python Monte Carlo Simulation Stochastic Optimization Probability Modeling Statcast Data Analysis

The Problem

Baseball managers must decide when to deploy their strongest relief pitchers during high-leverage situations. Rather than assuming the closer should automatically pitch the ninth inning, this model evaluates whether using the best pitcher earlier can improve the probability of winning.

The Approach

I created a simulation of the eighth and ninth innings that models batter outcomes, pitcher effectiveness, base runners, lineup position, and scoring. Thousands of simulated games are then used to compare bullpen strategies.

KEY FINDING

The optimal bullpen strategy changes depending on where the opposing team is in its batting order.

Simulations showed that using the closer in the eighth inning can outperform the traditional ninth-inning strategy when stronger hitters are due up earlier, demonstrating the value of leverage-based bullpen management.

How It Works
