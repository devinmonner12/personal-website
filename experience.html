Experience

Air Force Research Laboratory

VICEROY MAVEN Research Intern

Rome, New York

June 2026 - August 2026

During the summer of 2026, I participated in the Air Force Research Laboratory's VICEROY MAVEN internship program, where I worked on research projects supporting national defense initiatives in collaboration with engineers, researchers, and fellow interns.

Featured Project

DARPA Project LongShot

Working alongside my mentor and another intern, I supported NASA Ames Research Center's requirements analysis efforts for DARPA's Project LongShot, an advanced unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) program.

Our team reviewed and cross-referenced technical documentation provided by General Atomics, analyzed system requirements, and produced deliverables that contributed to the formalization and refinement of the project's requirements.

Threat Analysis

Cyber Vulnerability Assessment

As part of a multidisciplinary team, I conducted a cyber vulnerability assessment of a foreign AI-enabled data center hub. We analyzed the region's infrastructure, technological capabilities, and economic factors to evaluate potential vulnerabilities and produced an intelligence-style Cyber Blue Book summarizing our findings.

Throughout the project, I presented our research to program leadership and fellow interns, strengthening my technical communication and public speaking skills.

Recognition

Pentagon Visit

At the conclusion of the internship, I was selected as one of three interns from a cohort of more than 50 participants to represent the VICEROY MAVEN program at the Pentagon.

During my visit, I met with senior Department of Defense leaders and gained insight into the development of defense strategy, organizational leadership, and decision-making in one of the nation's most demanding and high-pressure environments.

Beyond the Internship

Mount Marcy

One of the highlights of my summer in New York happened outside the office. Along with two fellow VICEROY MAVEN interns, I hiked Mount Marcy, the highest peak in New York State, covering more than 15 miles through the Adirondack High Peaks Wilderness.

As someone from the Midwest, where mountains are hard to come by, I appreciated the opportunity to experience the Adirondacks and take on a hike unlike anything I'd done before.
